Syed Asad Ali Shah is a UK-based Islamic scholar and television host. Through Ziyarat with Asad Ali Shah he takes travellers to the sacred geography of the Ummah and explains, at each maqam, who is buried there, what the Qur’an and history say, and how to keep adab.

The work is both travel and teaching. Groups have visited Iraq — Baghdad, Najaf and Karbala — and the channel films ziyarat in Madaen, Jerusalem, Bosnia, Uzbekistan and the Two Holy Mosques so that knowledge reaches those who cannot yet travel.

Enquiries are handled from Peterborough. Write to {{ org.email }} or use the form. The Facebook page is followed by tens of thousands of people; YouTube and TikTok carry the films.
